# Introduction to rtylr

rtylr is the all-in-one business operating system designed for modern businesses. It brings together POS, ERP, HR, CRM, Finance, Flow, and Store into a single, unified platform.

## What is rtylr?

rtylr replaces the patchwork of disconnected tools most businesses rely on. Instead of juggling separate systems for sales, inventory, payroll, and customer management, rtylr provides a cohesive experience where everything works together from day one.

## Core Apps

- **Hub** — Central dashboard and team management
- **POS** — Point of sale for retail and restaurants
- **ERP** — Enterprise resource planning and inventory
- **HR** — Human resources and payroll
- **CRM** — Customer relationship management
- **Finance** — Accounting and financial reporting
- **Flow** — Workflow automation and task management
- **Store** — Online storefront builder

## Why rtylr?

Businesses waste time and money maintaining integrations between separate tools. Data gets siloed. Teams lose context switching between apps. rtylr solves this by building every module on a shared foundation with a unified data model, consistent UI, and seamless cross-app workflows.
