# Addon System

Addons extend rtylr's core functionality with optional features. They allow you to customize your setup and only pay for what you need.

## How Addons Work

Addons are self-contained feature modules that plug into one or more rtylr apps. When an addon is enabled, its features appear in the relevant app's interface. Addons are managed in **Hub > Addons**.

## Enabling & Disabling

Toggle addons on or off at any time. Some addons can be enabled per branch (e.g., KDS only at locations with kitchen displays) while others apply organization-wide (e.g., multi-language). Disabling an addon hides its features but preserves its data so it can be re-enabled later.

## Free vs. Premium

Some addons are included with all plans at no extra cost. Premium addons require a paid subscription (monthly or annual) and may have usage-based pricing. Check the pricing page or **Hub > Addons** for current pricing.

## Available Addons

### Menu Management

Advanced menu builder for restaurants with courses, modifier groups, combo meals, menu scheduling (breakfast, lunch, dinner), and seasonal menus. Integrates with POS and Store.

### Loyalty Program

Full loyalty engine with points, tiers, rewards catalog, and automated tier progression. Integrates with POS, CRM, and Store.

### Advanced Analytics

Extended analytics with predictive forecasting, anomaly detection, and custom dashboard builder. Adds drill-down capabilities to Insights.

### Custom Branding

Remove rtylr branding from customer-facing interfaces (receipts, Store, booking pages, emails) and replace with your own brand assets.

### Multi-Language

Translate your Store, booking pages, and customer communications into multiple languages. Includes interface localization for your team.

### Custom Domain

Use your own domain for your online Store and booking pages instead of the default `yourstore.rtylr.com` subdomain.

### Email Marketing

Send email campaigns with a drag-and-drop editor, audience segmentation, A/B testing, and delivery analytics. Integrates with CRM segments.

### SMS Notifications

Send transactional and marketing SMS messages to customers. Includes order updates, appointment reminders, and promotional campaigns.

### Reservations

Table reservation system for restaurants with online booking, floor plan integration, party size management, and waitlist. Works with POS floor plans.

### Online Ordering

Accept online orders for pickup or delivery through your Store. Includes real-time order tracking, delivery zone configuration, and estimated time calculation.

### Kitchen Display System

Digital order display for kitchen staff with station routing, order timing, bump functionality, and priority management. Connects to POS order flow.

### API Access

Programmatic access to rtylr data and operations via RESTful API. Includes webhook support, rate limiting, and scoped API keys.

### White Label

Fully rebrand the rtylr platform with your company name, logo, colors, and custom login page. Suitable for agencies and resellers.

### Priority Support

Dedicated support channel with faster response times, a named account manager, and priority issue resolution.

### Data Export

Bulk export your organization's data in standard formats (CSV, JSON). Includes scheduled exports and full data portability for compliance requirements.
