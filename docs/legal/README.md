# Legal readiness review — 19 September 2026

**Outcome: the repository does not establish that RTYLR is ready for unrestricted global operation.** This is an evidence-based product and policy review, not a legal opinion or certification. “Not found in this repository” does not mean an agreement or control does not exist elsewhere.

Reviewed `tryrtylr/rtylr` main at `034360d14`: landing policies in English, French and Arabic; authentication; recruitment collection; shared consent/observability; billing UI; source licenses. Backend operations, corporate records, vendor contracts, live production configuration, and customer contracts were not available in this scope. The requested market is global; the legal entity and incorporation country remain unconfirmed. Existing references to “Voxire” and Beirut do not establish either.

## Changes in this PR

- Correct the public security page in all three languages: remove unsupported infrastructure, audit, certification-timeline, patch-time and report-delivery claims. Correct processor breach-notification wording and restrict research to authorized accounts/data.
- Expand privacy coverage to employee, payroll and recruitment records; explain rights requests and cookie-choice withdrawal; avoid suggesting a formally appointed DPO through the contact heading.
- Add customer data-processing responsibilities, limitations of payroll/tax tools and preservation of mandatory rights to the terms. Preserve existing retention and billing commitments pending verification rather than silently weakening them.
- Put terms and privacy links immediately before email/social signup; distinguish contractual acceptance from marketing permission. This improves notice but does **not** add server-side proof of acceptance.
- Switch GA4 to basic consent mode: no Google tag or configuration before acceptance, disable collection and remove GA cookies on withdrawal. Remove the passive Google Preferred Sources embed, which loaded a third-party library regardless of choice.
- Keep PostHog listening for withdrawal and re-acceptance, disable autocapture, and stop starting Sentry Replay through a generic analytics choice. Reconcile changed choices on window focus.
- Add a review-only DPA draft and operational evidence templates below. These are not executed agreements or claims that the listed controls exist.

## Publication and contract change procedure

Before merging public text, reconcile existing customer agreements, confirm the operator identity and contact routes, and have the owner/legal reviewer approve the wording and translations. The existing Terms provide a 30-day notification period for existing customers; preserve the old version and send any required notices before treating amendments as effective. A page timestamp is not proof of notice or agreement. No customer notification or production deployment is performed by this PR.

For every public factual promise, retain an evidence link, owner, last verification date and expiry/review date. A published privacy notice is not proof that a contract is executed, a backend job runs, or an audit was passed.
