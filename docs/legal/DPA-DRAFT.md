# Data Processing Addendum — review draft

**NOT EXECUTED. NOT A PUBLIC OFFER.** This document supplies proposed language for a customer processing agreement. Complete every schedule and obtain legal/operational approval before offering or signing it. No production control, vendor agreement, transfer mechanism or corporate fact is established by this draft. This file is deliberately not linked from the public site as an available executed DPA.

## Parties and precedence

This addendum is between **[full customer legal name and address]** (Customer) and **[full operator legal name, registration and address]** (Provider), and supplements **[identified service agreement and date]**, effective **[agreed date]**. Contacts and authorized signatories appear in Schedule 1.

For Customer Personal Data processed on Customer's behalf, this addendum prevails over conflicting service-agreement terms. Mandatory transfer clauses prevail over both to the extent of conflict. The parties must expressly reconcile liability, audit costs and remedies with their service agreement; no clause may limit non-waivable individual rights or mandatory transfer-clause protections.

## Scope, roles and instructions

Customer is controller, or a processor authorized by its controller to appoint Provider. Provider is processor or subprocessor for the services and processing described in Schedule 1. Processing includes collection, storage, organization, retrieval, transmission, access, export and deletion only as needed for those services. The service agreement, this addendum, agreed configuration and documented authorized requests comprise instructions, including instructions for transfers.

Provider will process Customer Personal Data only on those instructions, unless required by applicable law. In that case Provider will inform Customer before processing unless the law prohibits notice on important public-interest grounds. Provider will promptly inform Customer if an instruction infringes applicable data-protection law and seek a lawful resolution. Provider will not use Customer Personal Data for its own advertising, sell it or train general-purpose models on it under these instructions.

Provider's separate controller processing for its own account administration, legal obligations and billing must be described separately in the approved privacy notice. This addendum does not convert an independent payment merchant's processing into subprocessing.

## People and safeguards

Provider will limit access to authorized personnel bound by confidentiality obligations and provide appropriate instructions and training. Provider will implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures actually specified in Schedule 2, taking account of risk, available technology, costs and the nature and purposes of processing. Material reductions in agreed protection require resolution with Customer before implementation.

Customer is responsible for its lawful collection, instructions, notices and access configuration. This allocation does not remove Provider's own statutory obligations. Sensitive information and children's data require an expressly documented, lawful scope and appropriate safeguards; do not assume a general SaaS agreement is sufficient.

## Subprocessors

Use the **[specific authorization / general written authorization — choose one]** model. List initial authorized subprocessors in Schedule 3. Under general authorization, Provider will give **[agreed advance notice period]** and sufficient details of intended additions or replacements so Customer can object on reasonable data-protection grounds before access begins. Document the resolution process, alternative service and termination option in Schedule 3; unresolved objections must not be treated as automatic consent.

Provider will impose equivalent applicable protection obligations through written subprocessor contracts, exercise appropriate diligence, and remain responsible to Customer for performance of subprocessor obligations as required by applicable law.

## Assistance and individual rights

Taking account of the processing and information available, Provider will assist Customer with access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objections and other applicable individual rights through appropriate technical and organizational measures. Provider will promptly refer requests concerning Customer-controlled data to Customer and not independently decide them unless authorized or legally required.

Provider will assist with security obligations, impact assessments, prior consultation and breach obligations as applicable. Agree contacts and response/service levels in Schedule 1 that allow Customer to meet legal deadlines; internal targets do not extend statutory deadlines.

## Personal-data breaches

Provider will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. The notice will give available details of the incident, affected data and people, likely consequences, containment/remediation and an incident contact. Incomplete details will follow in phases without undue delay; confirmation of every fact is not a condition for initial notice. Provider will preserve appropriate evidence and cooperate in response.

Customer determines its regulatory and individual notifications as controller, without limiting Provider's independent legal duties. Do not use a controller's potentially applicable 72-hour regulator deadline as permission to delay processor notice.

## Return, deletion and retention

At the end of processing, at Customer's choice, Provider will return or delete Customer Personal Data and delete copies, except retention required by law. Schedule 4 must specify usable export format/access, time windows, live deletion, backups, vendor propagation, legal holds and evidence. Retained copies must remain protected, purpose-restricted and deleted when the retention basis ends. Restore procedures must reapply deletion instructions. The schedule must reconcile existing contractual commitments rather than silently replace them.

## Demonstrating compliance and audit

Provider will make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance with applicable processor obligations and allow and contribute to Customer or authorized independent auditor inspections. Agree reasonable confidentiality, scheduling and security protections without frustrating legally required audits. Third-party assurance reports may support evidence but do not automatically replace all audit rights. Provider will inform Customer of material compliance issues and corrective measures.

## International transfers

Identify all processing and remote-access locations in Schedule 3. Restricted transfers require an applicable, valid mechanism and supporting assessments/measures before transfer. Where needed, execute the correct official EU SCC modules, UK instrument/addendum, Swiss adaptations or other jurisdiction-specific clauses with completed annexes and options. This draft does not reproduce, execute or substitute for those instruments. Do not claim execution simply by publishing a link to them.

## US state-law rider, if applicable

Counsel must assess the parties' business/service-provider/contractor roles and applicable state laws. An applicable rider should specify the limited business purposes; prohibit selling/sharing and impermissible use, disclosure or combination outside the direct business relationship; require the legally mandated level of privacy protection; provide rights to monitor and remediate; require notice of inability to comply; and bind onward recipients. Complete any required certifications and state-specific terms. This paragraph is a drafting instruction, not a completed CCPA/CPRA rider.

## Schedules — all must be completed

1. **Processing particulars:** parties/signatures; contacts; agreement; term; service modules; customer instructions; purposes; operations; categories of individuals (e.g. staff, candidates, merchant customers); data types; sensitive-data restrictions; frequency; rights/incident assistance channels and service levels.
2. **Verified measures:** identity/access/MFA; tenant isolation; encryption and key control; secure development; logging; vulnerability response; backups and tested recovery; deletion; staff confidentiality; access review. For each: deployed scope, implementation, evidence, verification date and owner. Do not copy aspirational controls into an executed schedule.
3. **Subprocessors and transfers:** legal entity, service, role, data, countries including support, contract, authorization, change notice and objection process, transfer mechanism, assessment and safeguards. Include a version and change history.
4. **Lifecycle:** purpose-specific retention, customer export format/window, termination, live/backup/vendor deletion, legal holds and verification. Reconcile the existing published 30/60/90-day terms and legally required records.

Obtain qualified review for the actual parties, destinations and services.
