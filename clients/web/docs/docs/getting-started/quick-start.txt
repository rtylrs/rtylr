# Quick Start

Get up and running with rtylr in a few steps.

## 1. Create your organization

Sign up at **rtylr.com** and create your organization. You will be assigned the Owner role with full access to all apps.

## 2. Choose your apps

From the Hub, enable the apps your business needs. Each app can be turned on or off independently. Team members can be granted access to specific apps based on their role.

## 3. Invite your team

Go to **Hub > Team** and invite team members by email. Assign roles (Admin, Manager, Staff) and choose which apps they can access.

## 4. Configure your settings

Each app has its own settings section. Start with the basics:

- Set your business name, address, and timezone
- Configure tax rates and currency
- Upload your logo and branding
- Connect your payment processor

## 5. Start using rtylr

Once configured, your team can start using rtylr immediately. Data flows between apps automatically. A sale in POS updates inventory in ERP, triggers commission calculations in HR, and reflects in Finance reports.
